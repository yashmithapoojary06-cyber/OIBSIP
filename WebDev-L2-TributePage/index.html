THE MAN BEHIND THE LEGACY

A Life Driven by Curiosity

Sir Chandrasekhara Venkata Raman was one of India's most celebrated physicists. His remarkable journey shows how curiosity, dedication and a passion for knowledge can create an impact that lasts for generations.

ABOUT HIM

From Curiosity to Discovery

🎓

Early Life

Born on 7 November 1888 in Tiruchirappalli, Tamil Nadu, Raman developed a strong interest in science from an early age.

🔬

Scientific Journey

Through observation and experimentation, he made important contributions to physics and encouraged scientific thinking in India.

🏆

Recognition

His extraordinary scientific achievements earned him international recognition and India's highest civilian honour, the Bharat Ratna.
