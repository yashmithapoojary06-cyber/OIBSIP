Sir C. V. Raman

The Scientist Who Revealed the Secrets of Light

A tribute to one of India's greatest scientists.

About Sir C. V. Raman

Sir Chandrasekhara Venkata Raman was one of India's most celebrated physicists. He was born on 7 November 1888 in Tiruchirappalli, Tamil Nadu. From a young age, he showed a strong interest in science and learning.

Raman made important contributions to the study of light, sound and the properties of materials. His most famous discovery was the Raman Effect, which demonstrated that light can change its wavelength when it interacts with matter.

His discovery earned worldwide recognition and made a major contribution to the field of physics. In 1930, Sir C. V. Raman received the Nobel Prize in Physics, becoming the first Asian scientist to receive a Nobel Prize in science.

Raman continued his scientific work and encouraged young people to develop curiosity and interest in science. His dedication, research and achievements remain an important part of India's scientific heritage.

Important Milestones

  1. 1888: Born in Tiruchirappalli, Tamil Nadu.
  2. 1928: Discovered the Raman Effect.
  3. 1930: Awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics.
  4. 1954: Received the Bharat Ratna.
  5. 1970: Passed away in Bengaluru, Karnataka.
